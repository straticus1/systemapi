03 / How it works

Your host connects out.
Your control stays central.

The Go agent opens an outbound WebSocket connection. The control plane routes approved work and receives execution results and telemetry.

Enroll a Linux host.

Configure the control-plane address, tenant identity, and agent credential.

Declare the work and its scope.

Submit a Sysscript with the entitlements required for the operation.

Inspect the outcome.

Use the returned result, telemetry, alerts, and audit verification to understand the change.
